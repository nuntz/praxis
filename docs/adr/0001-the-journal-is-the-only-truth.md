# The Journal is the only truth

Praxis is agentic-first, so much of what the person tells it arrives as Conversation with the Agent. We decided that the Conversation is not data: a Message is never an Entry, and something said becomes part of the Journal only when a tool records it as an Entry, in the person's own words. The Journal and what derives from it are the whole of a Deployment's truth, so the Conversation can be compacted, left out of backup and export, or lost in a restore without losing anything true.

## Considered Options

- **Every Message is an Entry.** Rejected: conversation is full of corrections and asides that are not journal material, and the Journal would fill with them.
- **Messages kept as a second record** beside Entries, searchable and backed up. Rejected: two places to look for what happened, and the more sensitive of the two would have to be kept forever.

## Consequences

- There is one continuing Conversation and no list of past ones; nothing of value lives only there.
- An Entry does not link back to the Message it was recorded from.
- What the Agent says enters the Journal only as an Entry with the Agent as Author.
- Whatever the Agent remembers across compaction must not be truth that exists only in the Conversation.
